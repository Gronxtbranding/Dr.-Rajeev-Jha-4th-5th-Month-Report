Strategic outcomes
The five developments that defined Months 4 & 5
  • Visibility & content performance. 3,875 impressions and 214 likes across Posts 13–20, holding a 6% average engagement rate, with a strong concentration of cybersecurity, GRC, AI-governance and digital-trust professionals engaging — including leaders from Comviva, KPMG and Devsha Tech.
  • Network penetration. 1,031 targeted connection requests sent to senior leaders across 59 organizations, yielding 362 confirmed connections — a 35% conversion rate.
  • Seniority quality. Of the 362 accepted connections, 298 (82%) sit at Director/Head level or above — 151 Directors/Heads, 133 MDs/Partners and 14 C-Suite/CXOs.
  • Inbound opportunities. Direct outreach surfaced a CISO / Enterprise Security Sales opportunity at Kapalins and an invitation to a Coursera executive roundtable on AI skills and workforce transformation — both arriving without outbound sales motion.
  • Market mapping. Outreach concentrated in global banks and consultancies — led by Morgan Stanley (112 targeted, 38 confirmed), Goldman Sachs (87 targeted, 45 confirmed) and Visa (101 targeted, 25 confirmed).
